Questions

The short answers. Message us for anything else.

Can my company logo go on the keychains?

Yes. Send the logo and it will appear in the 3D preview for you to approve.

Can each person get a different name and note?

Yes. Send a list with one name and one note per person and each keychain is made and packed for them.

Do you make Festival gift sets for teams?

Yes. Keychains come in an envelope with Festival wishes and a note of recognition. Send your list early so there is time to print and pack.

Can you paint a toy or figure I already have?

Yes. Include a photo and the colours you want in your order request, and we will reply with what is possible.

Can I order a gift with two names?

Yes. Couple sets carry both names, and kids’ gifts carry the child’s name.
